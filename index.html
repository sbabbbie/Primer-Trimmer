Sanger Primer Trimmer 5′ → 3′

Paste your assembled sequences and primers. Each sequence is checked in both orientations, primers are found (mismatches and degenerate bases allowed), and the trimmed sequences come out as FASTA.

Output
How it works
  • Enter both primers exactly as ordered, 5′→3′. The tool works out the reverse complements itself.
  • Each sequence is tried as entered (looking for F, then the reverse complement of R) and reverse-complemented (the same search on the flipped sequence). Whichever orientation matches better is used.
  • Degenerate bases (R, Y, S, W, K, M, B, D, H, V, N) work in primers and in sequences.
  • Mismatches are counted base by base. Insertions or deletions inside a primer site aren't detected, so check sequences flagged as missing a primer.
  • A cut-off primer match must be at the very start (F) or very end (R) and allows proportionally fewer mismatches.
  • Gaps (-), numbers and spaces in sequences are removed. Output is uppercase, one line per sequence.
